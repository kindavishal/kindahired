Why I built this

I didn't need another job board.

VVishalBuilt kindahired while looking for a new role

I've been looking for my next role for months. The hardest part wasn't finding jobs. It was keeping track of everything around them.

A recruiter would write to me on email. The hiring manager was someone I'd chatted with on LinkedIn. A friend had mentioned the team in an X DM. All of it was connected, but it lived in different places, and I couldn't see the whole picture.

So I was slow. By the time I'd read up on a company, fixed my resume and worked out who to message, the role had hundreds of applications ahead of mine. A recruiter I'd had a great call with went quiet over the holidays, and so did I. By January the role was gone. More than once, I remembered I knew someone at a company only after I'd applied cold.

I didn't need another job board. I needed my network, my inbox and the AI tools I already use every day to work together, so I could act on time. That's kindahired.

Questions

Want the longer version? See how it works, or where your data goes.

Does it apply or send messages for me?

No. It gets everything ready: who to message, what to say, your resume and cover note. You decide, and you press send.

Is it really free?

Yes, both ways. The AI work runs on your own Claude plan, so there's nothing for us to charge you for.

What do I need?

A Claude plan, a Gmail account, and Telegram on your phone. LinkedIn and X make it better, because that's where most of your network is.

What happens to my data?

It reads your Gmail and calendar without being able to send anything, and it skips messages that aren't about work. Nothing is sold, and there are no ads. See exactly where your data goes.

Why "kindahired"?

Because it won't promise you the job. It gets you ready, keeps you on time, and leaves the last step to you.
