Questions

Does it apply for me?

No. It opens the application with everything ready to paste. You press submit.

What if the fit read is wrong?

Every verdict shows its reasons. Build the pack anyway, or change the rules it follows in your playbook.

Which roles does it work for?

It started with community and DevRel roles. Your profile lists the role families you want, and screening follows it.

Why an app?

Packs and research are written by Claude Code on your own computer, on your own Claude plan. The app connects it. Everything else reaches you in Telegram.

Why "kindahired"?

Because it won't promise you the job. It gets you ready, keeps you on track, and leaves the last step to you.
