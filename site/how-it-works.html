Step by step, from a posting to an offer

AI makes the calls that need judgement. Plain code keeps the dates, status and reminders, so the parts that must be right every time are the same every time.

AIJudgementCodeBookkeeping

Find

Code
Spots new roles early

Watches the job boards of companies you name. A new role reaches you within hours of going live.

AI
Checks what could rule you out

Region locks, time zone, language, years asked. Skipped roles keep the reason, so you can overrule it.

Prepare

AI
Reads the fit, honestly

What fits, what's a stretch, and whether to apply at all. Never inflated.

AI
Prepares your application

Resume PDF, cover note and form answers, using only facts you've written down. Gaps become questions.

Reach

AI
Finds who's hiring

The likely hiring manager and recruiter, with the evidence for each, and a note drafted for both.

Code
Keeps every loop open

Replies you owe, follow-ups due, notes not sent. Each closes itself once the sync sees it done.

Land

AI
Preps the interview

One page with who you're meeting, every message about the role, your prepared application and a short checklist.

Code
Shows what's working

Which sources lead to replies, with the counts, and a note when the sample is small.

One queue, in order. Interviews first, then people waiting on you, then roles closing soon. The top task says why it's on top, and its button does the task.

Built to be trusted with your search

It prepares. You act.

It never applies, sends a message or follows up for you. Every send is your tap.

No invented facts

Every resume line traces back to your portfolio or your notes. When it isn't sure, it leaves the line out and asks you.

Honest over flattering

Some fit reads say "don't apply". That saves you an evening and a cold rejection.

Where your data goes

Your data and your tracker are kept in a private space of your own, apart from everyone else's. Applications and research are prepared on your computer, with your own Claude plan. No ads, no analytics, nothing sold. Privacy policy
