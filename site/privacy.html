Privacy policy

Last updated 28 November 2026

kindahired turns job postings into applications ready to send: a fit read, the apply link, a tailored resume, a cover note and people to contact. It prepares things; it never applies, sends a message or follows up for you. This page covers the kindahired website, the kindahired service (your dashboard and the Telegram bot) and the kindahired desktop app for macOS, Windows and Linux.

Questions and requests about your data go to hired@kindavishal.dev.

What kindahired collects

  • Early access requests: your email address and LinkedIn profile link, to decide on and send your invite.
  • Your account: your email address, used to sign you in with a one-time code.
  • What you give it about yourself: your profile, portfolio, notes and target roles, used to write resumes, cover notes and messages.
  • Google data, if you connect Google: read-only access to Gmail and Calendar, to find job alerts, replies from employers and interviews; and access to the tracker sheet kindahired keeps for you. Mail that isn't about work isn't stored.
  • Telegram, if you link it: your chat with the kindahired bot and the links you send it.
  • Job and contact information: the postings you track, and the people connected with them (names, roles and public profile links), used to prepare applications and outreach drafts.

Where it's kept and who can see it

Your data is kept on the kindahired server, in a private space of its own, apart from everyone else's. Other people using kindahired can't see it.

kindahired doesn't sell your data, show ads or use analytics or tracking.

Services your data passes through

  • Your own Claude plan: the desktop app runs Claude Code on your computer, signed in to your own Claude account, for fit reads, applications and research. What it sends to Anthropic is covered by your agreement with Anthropic. The app never reads or keeps your Claude sign-in.
  • Google: Gmail, Calendar and Sheets, as above. Some quick steps, such as screening job-alert emails, may run on Google's Gemini API; on its free tier, Google may use what's sent to improve its products.
  • Web search and posting sources: when a role has no usable link, kindahired may look it up with a web search service, and it may read public job boards and posts, including through the X (Twitter) API.
  • Telegram, for the bot; Cloudflare, which carries traffic to the kindahired server; and Gmail, which sends kindahired's own emails such as your invite.
  • App downloads and updates come from GitHub, or from the Microsoft Store for the Store version.

Google user data

kindahired's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide kindahired's features to you; it isn't sold, used for ads, or used to train general AI models. You can remove kindahired's access at any time from your Google Account permissions.

The desktop app

The app keeps a sign-in token for your kindahired account on your computer, encrypted with your system's keychain (Keychain on macOS, Windows' data protection on Windows, the keyring on Linux), and a few preferences. It takes Claude Code steps from your kindahired account, runs them on your computer and sends the answers back. It doesn't collect anything else from your computer.

How long it's kept, and deleting it

Your data is kept while your account is open. Email hired@kindavishal.dev from your account's address to get a copy of it or to have your account and data deleted; deletion is done within 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes what it keeps on your computer.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, you'll be told by email or in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
