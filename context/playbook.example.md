# Job search playbook

The standing instructions for every opportunity. Every automated step (sourcing, fit read,
resume, outreach, tracker updates) follows these rules. Copy this file to `context/playbook.md`
and edit it to change behaviour. Keep the numbered section headings: the tool reads the part
before "## 2." as a short summary of you.

## Who I am

- Based in [CITY, COUNTRY].
- Source of truth for every fact about me: my portfolio, [https://your-site.example], plus my saved notes.
- Resume format: PDF, unless I'm sending the website itself.

## 1. Find the role and check it properly

- Pull new openings from the job boards and alerts I've connected. Look at posts from the last 7 or 30 days.
- Find the exact application link on the company's own careers page, not the job board copy.
- Read the real job post. Note the location rules, visa or sponsorship needs, pay, and the questions on the form.
- Skip roles I can't take (for example, locked to a country where I can't work), and say why each one was skipped.

## 2. Honest fit read first

- Say what fits, what's a stretch, and any hard gate: location, language, years of experience, technical depth.
- Never inflate. If I shouldn't apply, say so.

## 3. Only true facts about me

- Every claim comes from my portfolio or my saved notes. Nothing made up.
- If something is unclear, ask me instead of guessing.
- Put `[FILL IN]` where only I know the answer.

## 4. Tailored resume per role

- One resume per role.
- Lead with the proof points closest to that job.

## 5. People and outreach: draft only, never send

- Find the best people to contact on LinkedIn: the hiring manager, whoever posted the job, the lead of the team the role sits in, or the founder.
- Research their recent posts and use one as the hook.
- Draft a connection note and a DM for each person. Never send them.
- Suggest a send time in their time zone.

## 6. Tracker

- Roles: details, fit read, apply link, form answers, next step, status, resume link.
- Outreach: contact, hook, drafted messages, "Sent?" status.
- Never overwrite my edits or older rows.

## 7. My voice

- Simple words and short sentences. Sounds like me talking.
- Always "I", never "we".
- No "passionate", no jargon, no gushing about the company.
- Name a gap in one honest line, then move on.

## 8. Location and relocation

- [Which countries I can work in, and whether I'd relocate.]
- Answer form questions honestly about location, work authorisation and sponsorship.
- Remote roles must fit roughly [8 AM to 8 PM] my time.

## Hard rule: I act, the tool prepares

The tool never applies, sends a DM, sends an email or follows up by itself.
It prepares everything and puts it in front of me. I do the action and mark it done.
